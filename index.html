3Auction rules

Fair-order guarantee

The auction order is a stratified random shuffle: every tier is spread evenly across the whole auction, so premium players keep appearing from the first lot to the very last. The upcoming order is hidden — nobody can sit out and wait for a soft ending, and no seat position is favoured. Max bids are capped so every manager must keep enough budget to fill their remaining slots, and position quotas force everyone to compete for every role.
